Faults and drone readings are simulated: each event draws hypothetical faults and the system has to find them. Hover any mark for exact values; the tables at the bottom hold every number.

4. Findings

  • Decisions are trustworthy on every grid. Over all settings, 100% of repair calls and 98% of inspection calls were real faults; at most 0.05 false crew calls per event.
  • Charging is the bottleneck, not the logic. Cutting the charge from 3 h to 15 min raises the mean from 7% to 49% of faults found in 4 h; a longer window does the same thing more slowly (27% at 12 h). More drones help less (12% with 8) and better sensors even less (10% at 0.9 detection).
  • The radius barely matters once the grid is covered. R changes the result only where coverage drops (mesh and corridor at small R or K = 3).
  • Bayesian search beats both patrols on Bavaria (real), Lyon, Texas, Norway, and still does when the risk model is wrong: it revisits evidence and focuses scarce sorties.
  • Where it loses: Tokyo. On a small dense grid the drones can see most of the line in one wave, so an even sweep with confirmation finds more than focusing on the riskiest cells. Next improvement: add an exploration bonus for unseen line when the total line is small compared with what the fleet can fly.
